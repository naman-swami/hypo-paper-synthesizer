# Systematic Review & Literature Synthesis Methodology

## PRISMA 2020 Compliance Standards
The Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses (PRISMA) statement guides systematic research synthesis:
1. **Identification**: Systematic query retrieval across arXiv, PubMed, and CrossRef.
2. **Screening**: Removal of duplicates, automated abstract claim extraction.
3. **Eligibility**: Full-text empirical verification against stated baseline benchmarks.
4. **Included**: Structured BibTeX provenance and evidence aggregation.
